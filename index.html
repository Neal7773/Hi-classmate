About Hi Classmate

Hi Classmate is a collection of original personality quizzes, social games, interactive stories and quick challenges.

Some games are inspired by familiar Filipino experiences such as barkadas, videoke, merienda and fiestas, while others explore relationships, personality, choices, emotions and everyday preferences.

Most games take only a few minutes to play. Choose your answers, discover your result and share it with friends to compare what everyone gets.
