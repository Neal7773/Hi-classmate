About The Devil’s Offer

Eight tempting deals promise something you might want at a price you might not accept. Love, recognition, a rewritten life and power all appear on the table. The Devil’s Offer asks where your curiosity ends and where a boundary begins, using fictional bargains rather than real-world advice.

How to Play

Read both the offer and its cost, then accept or refuse. Each deal stands on its own. After eight decisions, the result shows the temptation you followed most and the kind of value your refusals protected. Review your actual choices, share the headline result or begin again to test another set of boundaries.

Desire and Refusal Tell Different Stories

Accepted offers contribute to the temptation result. Refusals separately describe what you chose to protect, so two players with the same headline can have different personal explanations. Refuse every deal and the game returns Not for Sale. Accept everything and it explicitly notes that none of the eight prices stopped you in that playthrough. Tied patterns use the latest relevant decision. The shared card represents the headline, while your protected value remains part of your on-page reading. No outcome measures morality or predicts what you would do under real pressure; these are imaginary choices designed for conversation.
