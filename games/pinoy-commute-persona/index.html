Everybody Commutes Differently

A normal commute can change very quickly.

There may be a jeepney ride, a crowded train, unexpected rain, traffic that refuses to move or a barkada deciding how everyone is getting home.

Some people prepare for everything. Some simply adapt. Others somehow turn the entire trip into an adventure.

What’s Your Pinoy Commute Persona? turns familiar Filipino commuting situations into a short personality game.

Your Choices on the Road

The game gives you eight situations involving everyday travel, including jeepneys, trains, rainy commutes and trips with friends.

Each situation has two possible responses. Choose what you would most likely do.

Your combination of answers leads to one of eight commute personas. The result is a playful description based only on your choices in the game—it is not a judgment of how well or badly you actually commute.

Same Destination, Very Different Commuters

Two friends can take the same route and experience it completely differently.

One wants the exact route planned before leaving. Another is happy to figure things out along the way. Someone brings everything they might need, while another realizes what they forgot only after the trip has started.

Compare your commute persona with your friends and see who you would actually want beside you when the weather changes and the journey suddenly stops going according to plan.
