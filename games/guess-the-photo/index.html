VISUAL THINKING

Why close-ups feel tricky

Context guides recognition. Your brain normally uses the desk, hand, or classroom around an object to identify it quickly.

Edges carry information. Curves, corners, openings, and repeating patterns can reveal an object even when most of it is hidden.

Expectations can mislead. The first idea that comes to mind is useful, but comparing every choice often catches a better match.

This game is made for entertainment and friendly competition. It is not a vision, perception, or medical assessment.
