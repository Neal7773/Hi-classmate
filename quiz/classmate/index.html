About What Type of Classmate Are You?

Every classroom has its own mix of personalities. There is always someone who takes the group project seriously, someone who can make the whole class laugh, someone who knows everybody, and someone who somehow disappears when work needs to be done.

What Type of Classmate Are You? is a lighthearted personality quiz inspired by everyday classroom situations. The questions are about the small choices students make at school, from working with classmates to dealing with familiar moments during the school day.

Your answers are used to match you with one of six classmate personalities. There are no right or wrong answers. The quiz is made for fun, so choose the answer that feels most like what you would actually do.

How to Play

The quiz has eight questions. Choose one answer for each classroom situation and continue until you reach the result screen.

Your choices give different weights to the six possible classmate personalities. When the quiz is finished, the game compares those choices and selects the result that best matches your answers.

You can replay the quiz with different choices or share your result with friends and see whether they agree with your classroom personality.

Compare Your Result With Your Friends

The fun does not have to end with your own result. Send the quiz to your classmates or barkada and compare what everyone gets.

You might discover that the friend who claims to be the responsible one gets a very different result.
