Built for the group chat

Every friend group has a planner, a late legend, a screenshot keeper, and someone who appears only when food is involved. The funniest part is deciding which friend matches each result.

This game is for entertainment and friendly teasing—not a judgment about real friendships.

Every Barkada Has Different Roles

A barkada is rarely a group of people who all act the same.

Someone organizes the plans. Someone keeps everyone laughing. There is usually a friend people turn to when something goes wrong, and there may also be somebody who appears in the group chat only after all the decisions have already been made.

What’s Your Barkada Role? is a lighthearted personality quiz about the part you naturally play when you're with your friends.

How Your Barkada Role Is Chosen

The quiz contains eight two-choice situations inspired by everyday moments with friends.

Choose what you would most likely do rather than trying to aim for a particular result. Your pattern of answers is used to match you with one of eight barkada roles.

The results are playful personality types rather than serious psychological categories. There is no best or worst role.

Let the Barkada Decide

You may think you already know your role, but your friends might have a completely different answer.

Share your result with the group and let everyone take the quiz. Comparing results can be more entertaining than the quiz itself—especially when two people both believe they're the responsible one.
