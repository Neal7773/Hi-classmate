      About Build Your Dream Room

      Build Your Dream Room is an interactive decorating game where your choices become a space you can actually see. Instead of answering a list of abstract questions, you choose the walls, bed, lighting, desk corner, wall decor, cozy extra, window view and final detail. Every selection changes the same illustrated room, so your finished design keeps the combination you created.

      You might bring a gaming setup into a sage-green room, hang music posters above a canopy bed or pair a rainy window with snacks for friends. There is no budget, no timer and no rule that everything has to match. The point is to create a place you would enjoy spending time in.

      How to Play

      Choose Start Designing, then pick one of four items at each of the eight steps. Watch the preview update before the next choice appears. Use Back to revisit an earlier step: your selections stay in the room, and changing an item replaces that choice without adding extra points. After the final touch, you receive your completed room, a room identity and three observations about specific features you selected.

      Save My Room creates a PNG of your actual design on your device, where supported. Share on Facebook links to your room type’s illustrated card; it does not automatically upload your personal PNG. To show the exact design, save the image and share it yourself. You can also edit the finished room or choose Design Again for a fresh start.

      What Your Dream Room Says About You

      The six playful identities are Cozy Daydreamer, Neon Night Owl, Minimal Zen, Creative Chaos, Social Sunshine and Secret Sanctuary. Selected items contribute to these themes, and the combined pattern determines the headline. If two themes tie, the most recent relevant choice helps decide the result.

      Your room is never replaced by a fixed result picture. Two people can receive the same identity and still build very different spaces. Compare the furniture, view and finishing details as well as the title. These identities are original entertainment prompts, not psychological assessments or predictions about how you must live.
