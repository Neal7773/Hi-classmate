How Pinoy Are You?

Being Filipino cannot really be reduced to a quiz score, but familiar everyday moments can make a fun game.

How Pinoy Are You? uses ten simple two-choice situations inspired by Filipino habits, experiences and everyday culture. The goal is not to decide who is “more Filipino.” It is a playful way to see which choices feel familiar to you.

How It Works

Choose between two options in each of the ten situations.

Your answers build a pattern that leads to one of eight playful Pinoy results. There are no wrong answers and the result is not a serious measurement of nationality, identity or culture.

The game is designed for entertainment and for comparing answers with friends.

Compare Your Pinoy Result

Once you receive your result, share it with your barkada or family and ask them to try the same questions.

The most entertaining part may not be the final result at all. It may be discovering the one question where everybody suddenly has a completely different opinion.
