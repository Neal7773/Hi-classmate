The unofficial Filipino videoke personality test

From family reunions and birthdays to barkada nights, the microphone always reveals a personality. This game turns familiar videoke moments into eight playful, shareable characters.

No singing skill is measured here. The only requirement is lakas ng loob—or one friend willing to start the chorus.

What Happens When Someone Hands You the Mic?

Videoke is rarely just about singing. One person grabs the microphone before anyone else gets a chance. Someone needs three songs before finally agreeing to sing. Another person turns every performance into a concert, while somebody else is perfectly happy cheering from the table.

What’s Your Videoke Persona? is a playful Filipino personality quiz inspired by those familiar videoke moments.

The game puts you into eight different situations and asks what you would most likely do. Your choices are then used to find the videoke personality that fits your answers best.

How to Play

There are eight quick questions with two choices each.

Choose the answer that sounds most like what you would really do at a videoke session. There are no correct answers and you do not need to be a good singer to play.

After the final question, your choices are matched with one of eight videoke personas.

Who Are You at Videoke Night?

Your result is meant for fun, so the best part is comparing it with the people who have actually seen you near a microphone.

Share the game with your barkada or family. You may already know exactly which friend will refuse the mic—and which one will never give it back.
