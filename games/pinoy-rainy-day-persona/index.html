When the Rain Changes the Plan

Rain can turn an ordinary Filipino day into a completely different one.

A simple trip can become a commute decision. Plans may move indoors. Streets can become difficult to cross, brownouts can change what happens at home, and suddenly everyone is checking the weather and deciding whether going out is still worth it.

People handle those days very differently.

What’s Your Pinoy Rainy-Day Persona? uses familiar rainy-day situations to find out which type sounds most like you.

Eight Rainy-Day Decisions

The game presents eight two-choice situations involving rain, commuting, baha, changing plans and time spent at home.

Choose the response that feels closest to what you would normally do.

Your choices lead to one of eight playful rainy-day personas. There is no correct strategy and no result that means you are better prepared than somebody else.

This is an entertainment quiz about everyday habits, not a weather-safety guide. For real severe-weather decisions, always follow official local advisories.

What Kind of Rainy-Day Friend Are You?

Some people cancel immediately. Others wait five minutes and hope the sky changes its mind. Some are prepared with everything they need, while others discover the rain only when they are already outside.

Share your result and compare it with the people you usually make plans with. You may finally discover why deciding whether to cancel during rainy season takes so many messages.
