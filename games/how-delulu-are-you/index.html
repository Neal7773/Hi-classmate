Be Honest: How Delulu Are You?

A message gets a little longer than usual. Your crush watches your story. Someone reacts to an old post. Suddenly your brain has already written the next three episodes of a love story that may not even exist.

How Delulu Are You? is a playful quiz about those moments when imagination gets a little ahead of reality.

The questions are inspired by crushes, chats, social media and the tiny signals people sometimes overanalyze when they like someone.

Eight Situations, One Delulu Level

You will face eight two-choice situations.

Pick the answer that sounds most like your real reaction. Trying to choose the “sensible” option every time defeats the point—your first instinct is usually more fun.

Your answers earn points that are combined into a final score. That score places you into one of six playful delulu ranks.

The comparison percentage shown with your result is part of the entertainment experience. This quiz is not a psychological assessment and does not measure a real personality trait.

Did You See a Sign—or Create One?

The best part of a delulu quiz is comparing answers.

Share your result with your barkada and find out who stays calm after a simple message and who has already imagined the wedding because their crush sent two emojis instead of one.
