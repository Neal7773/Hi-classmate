YOUR AUCTION RECEIPT

What would you trade for a different tomorrow?

A voice saying they are proud of you. A last afternoon with an old friend. The song that feels like home. At this imaginary auction, ordinary memories can buy extraordinary futures.

Eight lots. Two sides of your story.

Choose Sell this memory or Keep it forever for each offer. Treat the eight lots as separate imaginary trades. Selling a memory scores the future it could buy: belonging, achievement, a fresh start or freedom. Keeping it scores what you held onto: growth, relationships, old dreams or everyday comfort.

Each future and memory theme appears twice. The most frequent sold theme and kept theme determine your result; a tie uses your latest relevant choice. Keep everything to become The Memory Keeper. Sell everything and your receipt explicitly says no memory here was a firm boundary. The result reflects these story choices, not a prediction about your real memories.

Review all eight decisions to see each price and your exact choice. Shared cards show your headline result; the personal memory theme is shown in your on-screen receipt. You do not need to enter names, upload photos or share any actual memories.
