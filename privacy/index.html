YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Hi Classmate handles when you visit our website and play our quizzes or games.

LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 1, 2026

Information you provide

You can play our current quizzes and games without creating an account. Your quiz answers and game scores are processed in your browser to produce a result and are not submitted to Hi Classmate.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message. We use it only to respond, investigate a problem, or handle your request.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security events. This information may appear in short-lived server or security logs.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and game performance. It may receive information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser details, traffic source, game starts, answers, completions, result types, retry actions, and sharing-button clicks. We do not send quiz answer text, names, email addresses, or Facebook account information to Google Analytics.

Cookies and local storage

Hi Classmate does not currently use advertising cookies or user accounts. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to distinguish visits and produce aggregated reports. You can limit analytics through your browser settings or Google's available privacy controls. Third-party services opened from our site apply their own policies.

Facebook sharing and external links

The Facebook share button opens a user-controlled sharing page on Facebook. Hi Classmate does not post automatically. Facebook and other external websites process information under their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

How information is used

  • Deliver, maintain, and secure the website
  • Diagnose errors and prevent abuse
  • Respond to messages and privacy requests
  • Understand general site performance when privacy-respecting measurements are enabled
  • Meet legal obligations and protect our rights

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help host, secure, or operate the website, or disclosed when required by law. We keep contact messages only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose they were sent and retain technical records according to operational and security needs.

Children and young users

Hi Classmate is a general-audience entertainment site and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Younger users should ask a parent or guardian before sending personal information online.

Your choices and contact

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information you sent directly to us, subject to applicable law. Email contact@hiclassmate.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line.
