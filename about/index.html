Hi Classmate is a growing collection of quick quizzes, personality tests, and mini-games designed to turn an ordinary break into something worth sharing.

Why we made Hi Classmate

School life is full of small moments that become the stories friends remember: the classmate who always has snacks, the seatmate with the perfect answer, and the friendly challenge that takes over the group chat. We make light, mobile-friendly experiences inspired by those moments.

Our goal is simple: give friends a fast way to play, compare results, and laugh together—wherever they are.

What you can expect

🎒Relatable fun

Classroom-inspired ideas that feel familiar to students and barkadas.

⚡Quick to play

Mobile-first games that fit naturally into a short break.

💜Positive by design

Results made for friendly conversation, never harsh judgment.

How our content is made

Each experience is planned, written, designed, and tested for Hi Classmate. Personality results are created for entertainment and do not represent psychological, educational, medical, or professional assessments. Skill-game scores can vary by device, attention, and environment.

Help us improve

Found an issue or have a game idea your barkada would love? Visit our Contact page. We welcome corrections and thoughtful suggestions.
