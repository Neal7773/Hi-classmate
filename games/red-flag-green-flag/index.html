Red Flag or Green Flag? is a fast dating-radar game built around ten relationship and social-media situations.

Instead of answering traditional quiz questions, you make a quick judgment about each situation.

How Good Is Your Dating Radar?

For every round, choose either Red Flag or Green Flag.

The game has a predefined Red/Green pattern for its ten situations. At the end, your choices are compared with that pattern to produce a Dating Radar score from 0% to 100%.

Your score then leads to one of four playful result levels, ranging from strong red-flag detection to mixed signals and very questionable radar.

There is no need to stop after every question to check whether you were “right.” Make all ten decisions first and see your result at the end.

A Game, Not Relationship Advice

Real relationships are more complicated than ten Red or Green buttons.

Context matters, people communicate differently and a short fictional situation cannot tell you whether a real person or relationship is healthy.

For that reason, the Dating Radar percentage is an entertainment score created by the game. It is not professional relationship advice or a psychological assessment.

Would Your Friends Agree?

The best questions are often the ones where people disagree.

Share your result and ask your friends to play without telling them what you selected.

Maybe everybody agrees on the obvious situations. Or maybe one question turns into a twenty-minute group-chat debate.

Either way, that disagreement is part of the game.
