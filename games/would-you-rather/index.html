Two Choices. One Decision.

Would You Rather? is built around one simple rule: you have to choose.

Each of the ten questions gives you two different options. Sometimes one choice may seem obvious. Other times both choices may be equally tempting—or equally terrible.

There is no correct answer. The fun comes from deciding what you would actually choose and comparing that decision with other players.

How to Play

Go through all ten questions and choose one of the two options each time.

Do not spend too long trying to find the “best” answer. There isn't one. Pick whichever choice feels more like you.

When all ten decisions are complete, your pattern of choices is used to produce one of eight playful results.

What Would Your Friends Choose?

Would You Rather games become much more interesting when people disagree.

Share your result or the game itself with your friends and compare choices. A question that seems completely obvious to you may be the exact question your friend refuses to agree with.
