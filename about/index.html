Meet Hi Classmate

Hi Classmate is a collection of original quizzes, personality tests, social games, interactive stories and quick challenges made for people who enjoy discovering a result and comparing it with friends.

Some games are inspired by familiar Filipino experiences—from barkadas and videoke to merienda, fiestas and everyday social moments. Others explore universal themes such as relationships, personality, choices, emotions, travel preferences and imagination.

Most games are designed to be quick to play. Choose the answers that feel closest to you, discover your result and share it if you want to compare with friends.

Hi Classmate’s personality results are created for entertainment and self-reflection. They are not professional psychological, medical or relationship assessments.

What you can expect

How our content is made

Each experience is planned, written, designed, and tested for Hi Classmate. Personality results are created for entertainment and do not represent psychological, educational, medical, or professional assessments. Skill-game scores can vary by device, attention, and environment.
